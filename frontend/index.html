How it works

Four steps from your phone or computer.

  1. Create your account

    Fill in your details and address. Staff review your registration to confirm residency.

  2. Request a document

    Select an available service and submit the purpose and payment details.

  3. Track every step

    See whether your request is pending, processing, ready, or completed.

  4. Collect it

    Receive an update when the document is ready for release.

Common questions

Who can register?

Residents of the barangay. Your registration is reviewed by staff before you can use resident services.

How is my residency verified?

Staff check the details and supporting documents you submit against barangay records.

How can I track a document request?

Log in to the resident portal and open Request Tracking to view the latest status.
